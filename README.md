# Sunrise Coffee Power BI Analysis

## Project Overview
This project is an interactive Power BI business intelligence dashboard developed to analyze Sunrise Coffee's business performance and generate meaningful insights.
The analysis focuses on revenue performance, profitability, store performance, customer loyalty, product contribution, and seasonal trends.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query
- Data Modelling
- DAX Measures
- Data Visualization

## Dashboard Pages
- Executive Overview
- Store Performance
- Loyalty Dashboard
- Revenue & Product Analysis
- Seasonal Analysis

## Key Analysis Areas
- Revenue and profitability trends
- Store performance comparison
- Customer loyalty performance
- Product category contribution
- Seasonal sales patterns

## Project Objective
To transform business data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision making.

## Key Insights
- Sunrise Coffee achieved an overall revenue of £89.69M and total net profit of £16.88M, demonstrating strong business performance across the analyzed period.
- Regional profitability varied, with Northern Ireland recording the highest net profit margin, while East of England and Yorkshire & Humber recorded the lowest margins, indicating potential areas for operational improvement.
- Store profitability changed over time. In 2023, Sunrise Coffee Aberdeen Express recorded the highest total net profit, while Sunrise Coffee Belfast Yard became the leading store by total net profit in 2024.
- Revenue generation and profitability did not always align. In 2023, Sunrise Coffee East London Lounge achieved the highest revenue, while Aberdeen Express led in net profit. In 2024, Cheltenham Quarter generated the highest revenue, while Belfast Yard recorded the highest net profit.
- Sunrise Coffee Cheltenham Quarter recorded the highest loyalty member revenue, suggesting strong customer engagement and loyalty contribution at the store.

## Recommendations
- Investigate the operational factors contributing to Northern Ireland's high net profit margin and identify strategies that can be replicated across lower-performing regions.
- Review the cost structures and operational processes of regions with lower profitability, particularly East of England and Yorkshire & Humber, to identify opportunities for improvement.
- Analyze the strategies used by high-profit stores such as Aberdeen Express and Belfast Yard to understand the drivers of strong profitability and apply successful practices across other locations.
- Continue investing in customer loyalty initiatives, using insights from high-performing locations such as Cheltenham Quarter to improve customer retention and engagement.
- Maintain a balanced focus on both revenue growth and profitability, as higher sales do not always translate into higher profit performance.
